Please find below the details regarding the serial number issue reported in the Smart Form output.

Issue:
Serial number was not appearing in the Smart Form for specific serialized materials.

Root Cause:
During 261 Goods Issue posting, the required serial number was not entered by the user. As a result, the serial number was not stored in SAP and could not be fetched by the Smart Form.

Action Taken:
Verified the affected transaction.
Performed root cause analysis.
Informed concerned users.
Started manual verification of impacted records.
